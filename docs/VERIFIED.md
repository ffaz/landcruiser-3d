## Corrections from the independent prompt audit (2026-11-03)
- **R3 tail-lamp lower section:** the SOURCE is smoked, the real car is clear. OWNER DECISION 2026-11-03: use the normal OEM (clear) lamp. This is an exception to source-is-truth.
- **Shut lines:** a real 4–5 mm gap is about 1 px at 1600 px side view (5000 mm ≈ 1150 px). The target is 1–2 px following the source curve, not 2–3 px and not "straight".
- **Lug nuts:** the official Method and KMC front photos show the 6-stud variants. The car is 5×150, so always build 5 nuts. A 5-nut finding against those photos is correct as built, not a defect.
- **Method windows:** the top and bottom windows are not wider than the rest. Match the photo's proportions.
- **real-roof-ribs-rails.png** is a Rhino-Rack product shot and doesn't clearly prove factory roof ribs. Judge the roof against the SOURCE; if the source has no ribs, don't invent them.
- **Source render** = bronze VX.R. Paint colour and badge text are not source targets (the car is a white GXR).
- **Shanaber hole count:** ours is 18, and the photos may show about 15–16. Count before changing.

## Method MR705: measured from the official front photo (2026-11-05, pixel analysis)
Radii are fractions of the outer lip edge R; angles run clockwise from 12 o'clock.
- **Windows:** 10 at a 36° pitch.
  - Short at 0/72/144/216/288°: r 0.59–0.74, about 19° wide at the inner edge, 22° at the outer edge.
  - Long at 36/108/180/252/324°: r 0.49–0.74, about 30° inner, 27° outer.
- **Layout:** black cap r ≤ 0.13; lug circle: the photo is the 6-lug version, and on our 5×150 it is fixed at 75 mm = 0.325 R (the earlier "~0.20" estimate was wrong; Codex correctly refused it); raised hub ring to 0.34; flat face 0.34–0.49; flat band 0.74–0.85; groove 0.86–0.88; lip 0.89–1.0.
- **Tone:** face about L 150 (light matte titanium).
- **METHOD lettering:** r ~0.41, in the inner flat band above and below the hub. NOT near the lip.
- **Checker miss:** an earlier checker passed M4 with the lettering on the outer land, and scored the wheel 6.5 while it rendered dark gunmetal (L ~60). Always put the real photo side by side at the same scale.
